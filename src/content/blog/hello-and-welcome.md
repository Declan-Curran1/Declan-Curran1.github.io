This is the inaugural post on the new version of my site, mostly so the
"recent writing" section on the home page isn't empty on day one.

I'm planning to use this space for two things:

1. **Short stories** &mdash; mostly sci-fi, sometimes very short, occasionally
   strung-together notes from a longer idea I haven't worked out yet.
2. **Working notes** &mdash; what I'm reading, what I'm building, what I think
   I've figured out from the climate / ML / data side of things.

If something here lands, please tell me. If something here is wrong, definitely
tell me.
