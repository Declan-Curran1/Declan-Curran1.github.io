Your gentle web lay steadfast
Filled to the brim with breakfast

Without a care in the world
Our journey, together shared, engine whirred.

You are perhaps, the only one to have seen me cry
The only one to have seen me try
The only one to have seen me fly

Even when I clean your web
You’ll rebuild, your thread of flow and ebb

And although I never see your legs
I know from your persistent web, of your dregs

Somehow I prefer it this way
Still dirty, but at least you’re here to stay

To the spider in my side view mirror
Your presence makes my drive much less clearer
My hands less steady, turns hard to steerer
Yet, how glad I am, to have a friend that is nearer
