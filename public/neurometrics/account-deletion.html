Delete your NeuroMetrics account and data

This page explains how to delete your NeuroMetrics account and all associated data.

Option 1 — In the app (instant, recommended)

  1. Open NeuroMetrics and go to Account.
  2. Tap "Delete account & all data".
  3. Confirm. Your account and all sessions, metrics, and mood check-ins are permanently deleted from the cloud and from this device. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 — By email

Email declan.curran@outlook.com from the address on your account (or with your account details) and ask us to delete your account and data. We will action this within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleted permanently: your account, all recorded sessions, all per-session metrics, mood check-ins, and any research-consent record — both in the cloud and on your device.

Retention: we do not retain copies after deletion, except where the law requires (we currently keep none). Backups, if any, are purged on their normal rotation.

See also our privacy policy. Questions? Contact declan.curran@outlook.com.
