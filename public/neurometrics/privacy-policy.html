NeuroMetrics — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 June 2026  ·  Last updated: 25 June 2026

NeuroMetrics ("the app", "we", "us", "our") is operated by Declan Curran of New South Wales, Australia. This policy explains how we handle your personal information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). Because NeuroMetrics handles health information, we are bound by the Privacy Act and the APPs regardless of our size or turnover.

Contact / Privacy Officer: declan.curran@outlook.com

1. What NeuroMetrics is (and isn't)

NeuroMetrics is a wellness and self-insight tool. It estimates focus, a "Flow Score", and a mood reading from a consumer EEG headband (Muse). These are personalised wellness estimates, not medical or diagnostic measures. NeuroMetrics is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.

2. The information we collect

You can use the core app fully on-device without an account. Cloud features (sync, history, comparison to your average) are optional and only begin when you choose to sign in.

a) Account information (only if you create an account): email and password, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth); passwords are stored only as a one-way hash. Choosing "Continue anonymously" collects no email — only a randomly generated identifier.

b) Health and wellness information (sensitive information): when you record while signed in, we store a downsampled summary (about one point every 5 seconds) of EEG-derived brain-state metrics (focus, calm, engagement, fatigue, Flow Score), mood estimates (valence, arousal, 0–100 score), heart rate and HRV, experimental fNIRS oxygenation, session metadata (duration, headband model, optional "intention" label), and any optional mood check-ins. We do not upload your raw EEG waveform.

c) Stored only on your device (not uploaded): calibration baseline, settings, and the full local copy of recorded sessions.

d) Technical information: standard network information needed to communicate securely with our backend, handled transiently by our host. No advertising identifiers; the app contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Bluetooth & location: Bluetooth is used solely to connect to your headband. On Android 12+ scanning uses the "neverForLocation" flag; we do not collect or use your location. Older Android versions require a location permission to permit any Bluetooth scan, but we still do not access your location.

3. Sensitive information and consent (APP 3)

Brain, heart, and mood data are "health information", a type of sensitive information. We collect it only with your consent, given by choosing to record and (for cloud storage) by signing in. You can withdraw consent at any time by recording locally, signing out, or deleting your account and data.

4. How we use your information

  • To provide the app's features (live metrics, saving sessions, history, and comparing your Flow Score to your own historical average).
  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.
  • Optional research (opt-in only): only if you switch on "Help improve the models", your de-identified data may be used to improve our wellness models. Off by default; revocable anytime.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

5. Disclosure to third parties

We do not disclose your personal information except to our infrastructure provider, Supabase (database and authentication, as a processor under contract), or where required or authorised by law. Supabase is our only sub-processor — we use no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

6. Cross-border disclosure (APP 8)

Our backend database stores your data in Australia (AWS ap-southeast-2). Some operational metadata — such as authentication and system logs handled by our provider — may be processed overseas. By using cloud features you consent to storage and processing in these locations. Where data is held outside Australia, we take reasonable steps to ensure APP-consistent handling.

7. Security and retention (APP 11)

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by our host, and is restricted per-user by database row-level security. Your cloud metrics are stored pseudonymously — keyed to your account identifier and held separately from any identifying account details. Our infrastructure provider (Supabase) maintains recognised security controls, including SOC 2 compliance. We keep your data until you delete it or your account; deletion is permanent.

8. Access, correction, and deletion (APP 12 & 13)

View your history in-app and export any session as CSV or JSON. Contact us to correct information we hold. To delete everything, open Account → "Delete account & all data" in the app, which permanently removes your account and all cloud and on-device data — or email us to request deletion, which we action within 30 days. See our account & data deletion page for details.

9. Anonymity (APP 2)

You may use the app without identifying yourself ("Continue anonymously"), or by not creating an account at all.

10. Children

NeuroMetrics is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children.

11. Data breaches

If a breach likely to cause serious harm occurs, we will notify affected individuals and the OAIC as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

12. Complaints

Contact us first. If unsatisfied, you may complain to the OAIC at www.oaic.gov.au or 1300 363 992.

13. Changes
