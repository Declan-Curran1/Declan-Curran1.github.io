NeuroMetrics

Your Muse headband, now a deep-work coach. See when you're in flow.

Get it on Google Play

Requires a Muse 2 or Muse S EEG headband (sold separately) and an Android phone with Bluetooth.

How it works

  1. Calibrate once. A short guided session with voice, visuals and a breathing pacer learns your personal baseline, so your scores are yours, not an average.
  2. Work as normal. A quiet gauge shows your Flow Score (0–100) without pulling you out of the task.
  3. Review. Every session ends with a clear report: time in focus, dips and recovery.

What you get

  • A live Flow Score, built from focus, engagement and steadiness
  • Focus sessions with an intention and a timebox
  • Mood check-ins that tune your readings over time
  • Heart rate on supported headbands
  • An optional account with cloud sync, or keep everything on your phone

Honest science

No consumer headband can detect flow directly, and we won't pretend otherwise. The Flow Score estimates the conditions researchers link to flow: engaged, steady, moderately aroused attention. Every metric in the app shows how it's calculated. See the research behind each metric.

Privacy

NeuroMetrics works fully on your device without an account. If you choose to sync, your data is encrypted in transit, never sold or used for advertising, and you can delete it at any time.
